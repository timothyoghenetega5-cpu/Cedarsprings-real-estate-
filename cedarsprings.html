Our Story

A Visionary
Nigerian Agribusiness

Cedarsprings Global Resources was founded with a singular vision — to redefine what African agribusiness looks like at a global standard. Rooted in the fertile lands of Oyo State, we have built an enterprise that combines traditional livestock knowledge with cutting-edge agricultural systems.

Our farm operations at Labua Village and our administrative hub in Ibadan serve as the twin pillars of an expanding agricultural enterprise trusted by corporate buyers, institutional partners, and retail markets across Nigeria.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver premium livestock and agricultural products through ethical, sustainable, and innovation-driven farming practices.

🌐

Our Vision

To become West Africa's most trusted agribusiness enterprise, connecting Nigerian agriculture to the global marketplace.

🌱

Sustainability

Every operation is designed with environmental stewardship and long-term ecological balance at its core.

📊

Innovation

We integrate modern farm management technologies to optimize yield, quality, and supply chain efficiency.
